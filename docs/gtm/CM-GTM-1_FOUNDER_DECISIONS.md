# CM-GTM-1 — Founder decisions

The 15 Wave 1 products are Founder-approved and are not an open decision in this document.

## Decisions required before prospect activation

1. Assign the human pipeline owner or owners.
2. Approve the qualification-score rubric and priority thresholds.
3. Approve which UAE business types should be addressed first.
4. Approve the manual outreach tone, sender identity, follow-up limit, and stop-contact rule.
5. Define the acceptable provenance standard for a public contact and decision-maker role.

## Decisions required per sample request

1. Approve or decline the request.
2. Approve the product, presentation, and maximum sample quantity.
3. Decide who bears sample and delivery costs.
4. Confirm point-in-time availability through an authorized human.
5. Approve the handoff method and owner.

## Decisions required per quote

1. Approve quantity and AED unit price.
2. Approve delivery fee and VAT treatment.
3. Confirm point-in-time availability.
4. Approve validity date and payment terms.
5. Approve the exact recipient and manual send.

## Decisions required for KPI evidence

1. Approve the evidence format for conversations, completed meetings, sent quotes, and customer commitments.
2. Approve the operating review cadence and KPI owner.
3. Decide whether an evidenced customer commitment is sufficient for the internal `orders` KPI; this system must not create or process an order.

Until the relevant decision is recorded, the corresponding action remains blocked.
